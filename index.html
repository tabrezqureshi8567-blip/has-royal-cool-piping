About Us

Built For Bulk. Run By Tabrez Khan.

A dedicated HVAC, VRF & AC piping contractor for bulk projects.

H.A.S Royal Cool Service specialises in HVAC, VRF, ductable and cassette AC systems along with concealed AC copper piping for hospitals, societies, residential towers, commercial offices, shops and interior fit-outs. Founded and run by Tabrez Khan, we work directly with builders, civil contractors, interior designers and building engineers who need piping and AC installation done right the first time — not revisited after the walls go up.

Every project runs on the same standard: heavy-grade Mandev copper, nitrogen pressure testing, and a crew that keeps to your site schedule — whether it's a single flat or a full hospital or society block.
