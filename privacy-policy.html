Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how H.A.S Royal Cool Service ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website, use our booking form, or contact us by phone or WhatsApp. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

H.A.S Royal Cool Service provides AC and home appliance repair, and commercial AC installation, across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad. The business is run by Mohammad Tabrez Qureshi. Registered address: Sarve No 125, Jadhav Pada, Gaondevi Road, Dhaniv Talav, Palghar, Maharashtra – 401209. Phone and WhatsApp: +91 88286 34628.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: when you fill in the Book a Service form we collect your name, mobile number and the service you need. If you call or message us on WhatsApp, we also see your phone number and whatever you choose to tell us.
  • Automatic technical data: like most websites, visiting this site can create technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited and the time of visit. This is collected through the Google tag (Google Ads) installed on this website and through the Google Fonts service used for text styles.
  • Chat assistant: the on-page assistant answers from pre-written replies that run inside your own browser. What you type into it is not sent to our servers.

We do not ask for bank, card, Aadhaar or other government ID details on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To call you back, confirm your slot and send a technician for the service you requested.
  • To give you a cost estimate and provide follow-up support for the work we do.
  • To understand how many people contact us from our advertisements and improve our website and ads.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements where they apply.

4. Google Ads, cookies and similar technology

This website uses the Google tag for Google Ads conversion measurement. It may set or read cookies on your device so that we can count calls and booking requests that come from our ads, and so that Google can show ads that are more relevant to you. Google acts as an independent data controller for this; see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can control personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop you from calling us or booking a service.

We do not use your personal information to show sensitive-category advertising, and we do not collect sensitive personal data through the Google tag.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed:

  • Web3Forms (form processing service) delivers your booking request to our team by email.
  • Google (Google Ads tag and Google Fonts) receives technical data described above.
  • WhatsApp handles messages you choose to send us there, under its own privacy policy.
  • Our technicians and staff see your name, phone number and service request so they can serve you.
  • Authorities, if we are required to do so by law.

6. How long we keep it

We keep booking details only for as long as needed to complete and support your service, handle any follow-up or complaint, and meet legal or accounting requirements. After that we delete them or keep them in a form that does not identify you.

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time. To do so, call or WhatsApp us on +91 88286 34628 and we will act on your request within a reasonable time, in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as they apply to us.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No website or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults who book home services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

10. Links to other websites

This website may link to other sites such as WhatsApp or Google. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continuing to use the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
